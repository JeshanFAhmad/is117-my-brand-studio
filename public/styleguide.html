07 / Persuasion

Helpful, never pushy.

Two small choices invite exploration without pressure or saved preferences.

Reciprocity / a useful idea

“Start with the plain white tee and pieces you already enjoy wearing.”

Ungated styling advice gives a useful thought before asking visitors to choose a size.

Commitment / a chosen direction

“Keep it understated” or “Add a little expression.”

A visitor can choose a direction, see a matching idea, or change their mind. The choice is not saved and does not affect the size selector or demo bag.
