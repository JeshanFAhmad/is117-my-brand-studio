B / Postmodernist direction

Reference

Martine Bedin (for Memphis), Super lamp prototype, designed 1981; made by Fausto Celati. V&A, museum no. M.1-2011. V&A: What is Postmodernism?

Visual decisions

A tilted product panel, offset color blocks, and serif/sans contrast borrow the reference's repeated, brightly segmented silhouette as a cue for layering and mixed visual languages. The words and action stay in the same clear order as A.

Audience effect and risk

The visible layers may give the secondary audience more room for individuality and expression. Risk: extra visual energy could feel less calm to everyday dressers or compete with the shirt.
