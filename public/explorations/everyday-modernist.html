A / Modernist direction

Reference

Christian Dell, Dell-Lampe Type K, designed 1929; made by Zimmermann GmbH. V&A, museum no. M.27-1992. V&A: What was Modernism?

Visual decisions

A two-column grid, left-aligned type, and one restrained ink-on- paper palette make the product and action easy to scan. The reference's articulated lamp parts suggest separating the page into clear, purposeful units rather than adding ornament.

Audience effect and risk

The direct hierarchy may help everyday dressers find the message and shirt quickly. Risk: a sparse treatment can feel generic and may not signal the individuality the secondary audience wants.
